# DOVA — CEO Progress Update

**From:** Dozer  
**Date:** 25 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Executive update and decisions needed

---

Hi,

Here is the current position on DOVA.

The core marketplace is working and ready for an internal demo, on desktop and mobile. What is already built and running:

- Customer sign-up and login, with separate access for customers, suppliers, and admins
- Product catalog with search and product detail pages
- Shopping cart and checkout, with pickup or delivery
- Minimum order rules — ₦3,000 for pickup, ₦5,000 for delivery
- Order confirmation and order history for customers
- Supplier registration with document upload, pending admin approval
- Supplier dashboard — add and edit products with photos, manage stock, fulfill orders
- Admin dashboard — approve suppliers, monitor users, products and orders
- Contact form with an admin inbox for incoming messages
- Public pages — Home, About, Contact, and footer, in the DOVA brand
- Mobile-friendly layout across the whole site

DOVA is **not ready for public launch yet**. Two workstreams are still in progress:

- **Paystack:** checkout works in simulation, but the live account setup and real payment testing are not finished. No real customer money is being processed.
- **Feedlog:** still under development and not ready to present as a completed feature.

We also still need a shared staging link so the team can test it properly before any public release.

## Current phase

We are moving from core product development into **soft-launch preparation**.

The original four-week plan ends on **17 August 2026**. The customer, supplier, and admin journeys are already demoable, so the core product is in a good position. The remaining risk sits with Paystack, Feedlog, and final business sign-off.

## What happens next

1. Finish Paystack and Feedlog.
2. Put DOVA on a shared staging link.
3. Test the full flow there, including test payments.
4. Go/no-go decision for soft launch.

## Decisions and support needed

- Who owns the company Paystack account and can share access securely.
- Which suppliers will join the first pilot.
- Who should get the staging link for testing.
- Who approves the final About, Contact, and support details.
- Who makes the final launch call and the preferred soft-launch date.

## Recommendation

Continue internal demos of the core marketplace, but do not announce DOVA as publicly live and do not promise Paystack or Feedlog as finished yet.

Once Paystack, Feedlog, and staging testing are done, I will send a final launch-readiness update for the go/no-go decision.

— Dozer
